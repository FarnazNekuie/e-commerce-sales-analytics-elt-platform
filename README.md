## Analytics Dashboard

The [interactive Looker Studio dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/5748ab4b-ea3c-4a78-bd9b-3373faa4ef5e) connects to analytics marts in Snowflake and presents sales, product, operational, and customer insights.

### Sales Overview

Tracks total revenue, orders, items sold, average order value, delivery performance, review scores, and daily revenue trends.

![Sales Overview](screenshots/dashboard/sales-overview.png)

### Product Category Performance

Compares product-category revenue, sales volume, average price, freight value, and price-versus-volume behavior.

![Product Category Performance](screenshots/dashboard/product-category-performance.png)

### Customer Analytics

Analyzes customer acquisition, repeat-customer behavior, lifetime revenue, customer segments, and high-value customers.

![Customer Analytics](screenshots/dashboard/customer-analytics.png)
